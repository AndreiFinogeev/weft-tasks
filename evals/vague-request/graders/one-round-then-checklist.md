This is a wish with no defined finished state, and it is expensive to get
wrong — it ships publicly. The skill's core behaviour must appear.

PASS when the response:
- asks its questions in ONE message, not spread across turns;
- asks at most THREE questions;
- attaches a default to every question, so "defaults are fine" is a complete
  answer;
- promises or produces an acceptance checklist ("Done when …"), not a prose
  specification.

FAIL when the response: asks a fourth question; drips questions one at a time;
asks a question with no default offered; or skips straight to building the page
without establishing what "done" means.

A response that asks zero questions and instead writes a checklist with stated
assumptions is also a PASS — the skill permits proceeding on defaults.
